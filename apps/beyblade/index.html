How ratings work

Scores are coach estimates for deck planning — not official Takara Tomy ratings. Meta shifts; always cross-check live tournament data before a major event.

  • Competitiveness (0–100) — weighted part tiers (blade 50% + ratchet 25% + bit 25%) plus synergy bonuses for documented best partners, minus mismatch penalties (tall/weak ratchets, role clashes).
  • Meta-breaking (0–100) — disruptiveness (left-spin, expand blades, unusual role stacks), capped so low-tier random trash cannot score high.
  • Value for price (0–100) — performance proxy ÷ estimated HKD mid-range cost, normalized. HK prices are ranges / estimates from sealed retail anchors and MSRP-derived notes — not verified live listings.
  • Overall grade S–D — mostly competitiveness, lightly nudged by value.

See score.js and README.md for the tunable weight constants.
