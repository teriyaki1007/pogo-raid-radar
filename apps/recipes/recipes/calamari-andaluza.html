Calamari a la Andaluza

2 servings ~10 min prep ~10 min cook ~20 min total Quantities estimated

Fresh calamari rings, lightly salted and dusted in flour, shaken of excess, then fried in hot olive oil until golden — finished with lemon. Simple Andalusian fried squid, tapas-style.

Source note. Inspired by @cookingwithuri Instagram reel. Exact amounts weren’t listed — proportions below are sensible home estimates for 2 servings.
Approximate / not an official recipe. Classic Andalusian fried calamari method with sensible amounts for 2 servings. Adjust salt and lemon to taste.

Ingredients

Estimates for 2 servings (tapas / light main).

  • 400–500 g fresh calamari (squid), cleaned and cut into rings (tentacles optional)
  • 80–100 g plain flour (or a 50/50 flour + fine semolina mix for extra crunch)
  • 1–1½ tsp fine salt (plus more to finish)
  • 250–350 ml olive oil, for frying (enough for a shallow fry / 2–3 cm depth)
  • 1–2 lemons, cut into wedges
  • Optional: pinch of black pepper or smoked paprika in the flour; parsley to garnish

Method

  1. Pat dry. Rinse calamari if needed, then pat very dry with paper towels — moisture is the enemy of a crisp coat.
  2. Salt. Season the rings lightly with salt and toss. Let sit 2–3 minutes while you heat the oil.
  3. Light flour. Mix flour with a pinch of salt (and pepper/paprika if using). Toss calamari in the flour until just coated. Shake off excess thoroughly — you want a thin dusting, not a batter.
  4. Fry hot. Heat olive oil in a wide pan to ~180°C (sizzles instantly on a flour speck). Fry rings in batches 1–2 minutes until pale golden — don’t overcrowd or overcook (they turn rubbery). Drain on a rack or paper towels.
  5. Lemon and serve. Finish with a pinch of salt while hot. Serve immediately with lemon wedges (and parsley if you like).

Tips

  • Dry squid + hot oil + short fry = tender rings; long fry = chewy.
  • Shake excess flour well so the oil stays clean and the crust stays light.
  • Work in small batches so the oil temperature doesn’t crash.
