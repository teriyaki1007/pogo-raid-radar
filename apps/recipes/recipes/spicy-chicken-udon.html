10-Minute Spicy Chicken Udon (麻辣雞絲烏冬)

~2 servings ~10 min Cold toss Quantities estimated

Chewy udon tossed with shredded chicken, crab sticks, cool cucumber, and a spicy sesame–chili–vinegar sauce, finished with soft-boiled eggs — weeknight-fast and boldly seasoned.

Source note. Inspired by @cccooocccooo_1 Instagram reel. Caption and overlays did not list exact amounts — quantities below are sensible home-cook estimates matching the video assembly.
Approximate / not an official recipe. Reconstructed from reel visuals (udon, shredded chicken, crab sticks, cucumber, sauces, eggs). Adjust chili oil, vinegar, and sesame paste to taste.

Ingredients

Estimates for 2 servings.

Bowl

  • 2 servings fresh or frozen udon (≈400–450 g)
  • 1½ cups cooked shredded chicken (leftover roast or poached)
  • 4–6 crab sticks (imitation crab), shredded or torn
  • 1 Persian cucumber (or ½ English), julienned
  • 2 eggs (boil 6 min for small / 7 min for large), halved

Sauce (to taste)

  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1½–2 Tbsp sesame paste (or tahini thinned with a splash of water)
  • 1–1½ Tbsp Chinkiang / black vinegar
  • 1–2 Tbsp chili oil (with sediment), or to heat preference
  • Optional: ½ tsp sugar, pinch of salt, minced garlic

Method

  1. Boil the eggs. Soft-boil eggs — about 6 minutes for small eggs, 7 minutes for large — then ice bath, peel, and halve.
  2. Prep the cucumber. Julienne the cucumber into thin matchsticks. Set aside.
  3. Cook the udon. Boil udon according to package directions until chewy. Drain well (rinse briefly with cool water if serving cold-style).
  4. Build the bowl. In a wide mixing bowl, add shredded chicken and crab sticks, then the cooked udon and julienned cucumber.
  5. Sauce and toss. Add soy sauce, sesame paste, black vinegar, and chili oil. Toss vigorously until noodles are glossy and evenly coated. Taste and adjust heat, salt, or acidity.
  6. Finish. Divide into bowls and top with halved soft-boiled eggs. Serve immediately.

Tips

  • Thin sesame paste with a teaspoon of warm water if it’s too stiff to coat noodles.
  • Chili oil with sediment gives more flavor depth than plain chili oil alone.
  • Rotisserie chicken makes this truly 10-minute.
  • Keep cucumber crisp — add it last before tossing if prepping ahead.
