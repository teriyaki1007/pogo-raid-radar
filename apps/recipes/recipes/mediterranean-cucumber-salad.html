Mediterranean Cucumber Salad

~2 servings ~12 min prep No cook ~12 min total Quantities estimated

Crisp cucumber with leafy greens and herbs, sharp red onion, bright lime, and crumbly white cheese — tossed or shaken in a bowl until everything is glossy and fresh.

Source note. Inspired by an overall.eats Instagram reel. The caption gated official amounts — quantities below are sensible home-cook estimates. Comment RECIPE on the reel for the creator’s version.
Approximate / not an official recipe. Reconstructed from reel visuals (cucumber, greens/herbs, red onion, lime, feta-style cheese). Adjust salt, acid, and cheese to taste.

Ingredients

Estimates for 2 servings.

Salad

  • 2 large cucumbers, halved lengthwise and sliced into half-moons (or ribbons)
  • 2 large handfuls leafy greens or soft herbs (parsley, dill, mint, or mixed)
  • ½ small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 80–100 g (≈3 oz) feta or other crumbly white cheese
  • Optional: handful cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Optional: a few Kalamata olives, pitted and halved

Lime dressing

  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1½–2 Tbsp fresh lime juice (about 1 lime)
  • ½ tsp honey or a pinch of sugar (optional, to balance)
  • ½ small garlic clove, grated (optional)
  • ½ tsp dried oregano or za’atar
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Prep the vegetables. Wash and slice cucumbers. Thinly slice red onion (soak 5 minutes in cold water if you want it milder). Roughly chop leafy greens/herbs.
  2. Shake the dressing. In a jar or small bowl, combine olive oil, lime juice, honey, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Shake or whisk until emulsified. Taste — it should be bright and a little salty.
  3. Toss the bowl. Add cucumber, greens/herbs, and red onion to a wide bowl. Pour over most of the dressing and toss (or cover and shake) until everything is lightly coated.
  4. Finish with cheese. Crumble feta over the top. Add tomatoes or olives if using. Drizzle remaining dressing. Serve immediately while cucumber is still crisp.

Tips

  • Pat cucumber dry so the dressing clings instead of watering down.
  • Crumbled feta at the end keeps distinct salty pockets — don’t over-mix.
  • Swap lime for lemon if that’s what you have; keep the acid bright.
