Spam Musubi

~4–6 pieces ~15 min prep ~20 min cook ~35 min total

Pan-fried Spam glazed in a sweet-savory musubi sauce, pressed with short-grain sushi rice and furikake, then wrapped in nori — classic portable Hawaiian bites.

Source note. Full recipe from @dalumpiabros Instagram reel — caption includes complete ingredients and directions (not approximate).

Ingredients

Musubi

  • 1 can Spam, sliced into even pieces
  • Short-grain sushi rice
  • Furikake seasoning
  • Nori sheets (cut in half)
  • Plastic wrap

Musubi sauce

  • 1 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 Tbsp mirin

Method

  1. Slice and fry the Spam. Slice Spam into even pieces. Pan-fry until lightly browned on both sides.
  2. Cook the rice. Cook short-grain sushi rice according to package directions (or your usual sushi-rice method). Keep warm and sticky.
  3. Mix the sauce. Stir together brown sugar, soy sauce, and mirin until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Glaze the Spam. Pour the sauce over the fried Spam in the pan. Cook until the sauce reduces and coats each slice.
  5. Line the can. Line the empty Spam can with plastic wrap (leave overhang so you can lift later).
  6. Press the rice. Add about ½-inch of rice into the lined can; press gently so it holds together.
  7. Layer furikake and Spam. Sprinkle furikake over the rice, then place one glazed Spam slice on top.
  8. Unmold. Lift the stack out using the plastic wrap overhang.
  9. Wrap and serve. Wrap with a strip of nori (half sheet). Enjoy warm or at room temperature.

Tips

  • Use the empty Spam can as a mold so every piece matches the classic musubi shape.
  • Press rice firmly enough to hold, but don’t mash it into paste.
  • Wet fingers slightly if rice sticks while packing.
  • Sauce should be sticky and glossy — reduce over medium heat so it clings to the Spam.
