20-Minute Hoisin Garlic Noodles

~20 min 1 skillet ~2 servings

Wide knife-cut noodles tossed in a sticky hoisin-soy-sesame sauce with browned garlic and green onion, finished with chili oil and sesame seeds.

Source note. From @bisongainz Instagram reel — full ingredients and method as listed in the caption (caption also invites “comment recipe” for a DM copy).

Ingredients

Quantities as listed in the caption.

Noodles & aromatics

  • 8 oz knife-cut noodles
  • 4 garlic cloves (diced)
  • Green onion
  • Chili oil (to finish)
  • Sesame seeds (to finish)

Sauce

  • 2 tbsp hoisin
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp honey

Method

  1. Boil the noodles. Cook the knife-cut noodles, pulling them just before they’re fully cooked, then set aside.
  2. Whisk the sauce. In a bowl, whisk together hoisin, soy sauce, sesame oil, and honey.
  3. Brown the aromatics. Sauté diced garlic and green onion until browned.
  4. Toss. Pour the sauce into the pan, add the noodles, and toss until everything is coated.
  5. Finish. Top with chili oil, more green onion, and sesame seeds. Serve hot.

Tips

  • Pull noodles early so they finish cooking in the sauce without going mushy.
  • Brown the garlic gently — bitter burnt garlic will take over the hoisin.
  • Dial heat with chili oil at the end rather than cooking it into the sauce.
