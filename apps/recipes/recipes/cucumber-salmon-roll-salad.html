Cucumber Salmon Roll Salad

~2 servings ~20 minutes Quantities estimated

Thin cucumber ribbons wrapped around smoked or sashimi-grade salmon with a creamy sesame filling — served sliced like rolls or tumbled as a composed salad. Fresh, light, and weeknight-friendly.

Source note. Inspired by an overall.eats Instagram reel. The on-platform caption gated the real recipe, so amounts below are reasonable kitchen estimates — not a transcription.
Approximate / not an official recipe. Adjust salt, heat, and richness to taste. Use sushi-safe salmon if eating raw; otherwise choose smoked or cooked salmon.

Ingredients

Estimates for 2 generous servings.

  • 1 large English cucumber (or 2 Persian), for ribbons or coins
  • 120–150 g smoked salmon or sashimi-grade salmon, thinly sliced
  • Optional base: 1 cup cooked sushi rice or 2 large handfuls mixed greens
  • 3 tbsp cream cheese, softened or 2 tbsp mayonnaise + 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar or lemon juice
  • 1–2 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Optional: ½–1 tsp chili crisp or chili oil
  • Optional finish: thin scallion, avocado slices, or pickled ginger
  • Pinch of flaky salt and freshly ground pepper

Method

  1. Prep the cucumber. Wash and trim. For rolls: peel into long thin ribbons with a vegetable peeler (pat dry). For salad style: slice into thin coins or half-moons.
  2. Mix the creamy layer. Stir cream cheese (or mayo-soy) with sesame oil and rice vinegar until spreadable. Taste — it should be savory, lightly tangy, not heavy.
  3. Assemble rolls. Lay a cucumber ribbon flat. Spread a thin smear of the cream. Add a strip of salmon. Roll snugly. Repeat. Slice into bite-size pieces if you like.
  4. Or go salad-style. Toss cucumber with most of the cream, fold in torn salmon, and mound over rice or greens. Less rolling, same flavors.
  5. Finish. Scatter sesame seeds, a whisper of chili crisp, and scallion or avocado if using. Serve cold.

Tips

  • Pat cucumber dry so rolls don’t slip and the cream stays put.
  • Smoked salmon is the easy weeknight path; sashimi-grade is for when you trust your source.
  • Chili crisp is optional heat — start small; it blooms fast on cold fish.
  • Make the cream ahead; assemble just before eating so the cucumber stays crisp.
