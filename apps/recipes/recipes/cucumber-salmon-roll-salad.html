Cucumber Salmon Roll Salad

~2 servings ~15 min prep ~8 min cook ~25 min total Quantities estimated

Cool cucumber ribbons under warm pan-seared salmon cubes, finished with creamy white and spicy-sweet reddish sauces, red onion, cilantro, and scallions — a composed salad bowl, not a no-cook roll.

Source note. Inspired by an overall.eats Instagram reel. The on-platform caption gated the real recipe — amounts below are sensible kitchen estimates. Comment RECIPE on the reel for the creator’s official version.
Approximate / not an official recipe. Reconstructed from reel visuals (cucumber bed, fried salmon, dual sauces, fresh herbs). Adjust salt, heat, and richness to taste.

Ingredients

Estimates for 2 servings.

Base

  • 2 large cucumbers (Persian or English), ribboned or thinly sliced
  • ½ small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced on a bias
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro leaves, roughly chopped

Salmon

  • 250 g (≈9 oz) skinless salmon, cut into bite-size cubes
  • 1 Tbsp neutral oil (avocado or grapeseed)
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder (optional)

Creamy white sauce

  • 3 Tbsp mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt)
  • 1 Tbsp sour cream (or more mayo)
  • 1 tsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced or grated
  • Pinch of salt

Reddish sauce

  • 2 Tbsp sriracha or chili garlic sauce
  • 1 Tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  • ½ tsp rice vinegar or lime juice

Finish (optional)

  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Lime wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Prep the cucumber base. Wash cucumbers. Shave into thin ribbons with a peeler or mandoline (or slice thin rounds). Arrange loosely in a wide shallow bowl. Pat dry if very wet.
  2. Slice the aromatics. Thinly slice red onion and scallions; roughly chop cilantro. Set aside for topping.
  3. Mix both sauces. Whisk creamy white sauce until smooth. In another bowl, stir reddish sauce until glossy. Taste and adjust heat, sweetness, or salt.
  4. Season and sear the salmon. Pat cubes dry; toss with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Heat oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high. Sear in a single layer 2–3 minutes without moving, then flip and cook 2–4 minutes more until just opaque with lightly golden edges. Do not overcook.
  5. Assemble the bowl. Nestle hot salmon over the cucumber. Scatter red onion, scallions, and cilantro. Drizzle creamy white sauce in ribbons, then zigzag the reddish sauce. Optional: sesame oil, seeds, and lime wedges.
  6. Serve immediately. Eat while the salmon is warm and the cucumber still crisp. Toss lightly at the table if you prefer everything coated.

Tips

  • Dry cucumber keeps sauces from sliding into a soggy puddle.
  • Nonstick + medium-high heat gives crisp edges without drying the cubes.
  • Make sauces ahead; sear salmon last so the contrast of warm fish / cool cucumber stays sharp.
