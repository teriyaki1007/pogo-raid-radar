Hot Honey Crispy Chicken Burger

Approximate ~20 min prep ~25 min cook Marinate + fry

Buttermilk-marinated chicken dredged in seasoned flour and cornstarch, fried until golden, then glazed in sticky hot honey and finished with a lemony mayo sauce.

Source note. Approximate from @_nada_dl__ Instagram reel — ingredients from caption; method inferred.

Ingredients

Quantities as listed in the caption (some items have no amounts).

Chicken marinade

  • Chicken breast
  • 300 ml buttermilk
  • Salt, black pepper
  • Garlic powder, onion powder, chicken seasoning, paprika
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • Hot sauce

Dredge

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • Salt, black pepper, chicken seasoning, garlic powder, paprika

Hot honey glaze

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Hot sauce
  • Salt, black pepper
  • Garlic powder, onion powder
  • Brown sugar, paprika

Sauce

  • Mayonnaise
  • Black pepper, garlic powder
  • Chopped parsley
  • A squeeze of lemon juice

Method

Approximate — caption lists components only; steps inferred from structure.

  1. Marinate. Mix buttermilk with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, chicken seasoning, paprika, egg, white vinegar, and hot sauce. Add chicken breast and marinate (refrigerate while you prep dredge and sauces).
  2. Dredge. Combine flour, cornstarch, and the dredge seasonings. Lift chicken from marinade, coat thoroughly in the dredge, and shake off excess.
  3. Fry until golden. Fry or air-fry the coated chicken until deep golden and cooked through. Rest briefly on a rack so the crust stays crisp.
  4. Hot honey glaze. Melt butter with honey, hot sauce, salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, brown sugar, and paprika until sticky. Brush or toss the hot chicken in the glaze.
  5. Mix the sauce. Stir mayonnaise with black pepper, garlic powder, chopped parsley, and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  6. Assemble. Serve the glazed crispy chicken as a burger with the sauce (and glaze). Add a bun and toppings only if you want — they aren’t listed in the caption.

Tips

  • Serve on a toasted bun with lettuce and pickles if you like — optional; not in the caption.
  • Double-dredge (marinade → dredge → brief dip back in marinade → dredge again) for a thicker crunch if you prefer.
  • Balance hot sauce vs honey in the glaze to dial heat vs sweetness.
  • Keep glazed chicken off a bun until serving so the crust doesn’t steam soft.
