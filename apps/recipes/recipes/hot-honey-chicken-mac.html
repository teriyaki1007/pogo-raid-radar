High Protein Crispy Hot Honey Chicken Mac & Cheese

4 servings ~20 min prep ~25 min cook ~45 min total ~688 cal · 84C / 12F / 63P per serve

Bite-size chicken seasoned, egged, and cornflour-coated, then air-fried until crisp and tossed in a sticky hot-honey glaze. Serve over a light evaporated-milk cheese sauce macaroni — high protein comfort food built for meal prep.

Source note. Complete recipe from @_aussiefitness Instagram reel — caption includes ingredients, method, and macros (not approximate).

Ingredients

Makes 4 servings. Macros per serve (from caption): ~688 cal · 84 g carbs · 12 g fat · 63 g protein.

Crispy chicken

  • 700 g chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • ~80–100 g cornflour (cornstarch), for coating
  • Cooking spray (for air fryer)

Hot honey glaze

  • 2–3 Tbsp hot sauce (to taste)
  • 2–3 Tbsp honey
  • Optional: squeeze of lemon

Mac & cheese

  • 320 g dry macaroni
  • 1–2 Tbsp light butter
  • ~340 ml fat-free / light evaporated milk
  • ~80–120 g light cream cheese
  • ~80–100 g grated light cheddar
  • ½–1 tsp of the same chicken seasonings (garlic, onion, paprika)
  • Splash of reserved pasta water, as needed

Method

  1. Season the chicken. Pat chicken dry. Toss with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  2. Egg and cornflour coat. Dip seasoned pieces in beaten egg, letting excess drip off, then toss in cornflour until fully coated. Shake off excess flour.
  3. Air-fry crisp. Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer. Spray lightly with cooking spray. Air-fry at ~200°C (390°F) for about 15–18 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through. (Oven bake ~200°C / 25 minutes also works.)
  4. Hot-honey glaze. Stir hot sauce and honey (and lemon if using) together. Toss the hot crispy chicken in the glaze until glossy and coated. Set aside.
  5. Cook the macaroni. Boil macaroni in salted water until just al dente. Reserve a splash of pasta water; drain.
  6. Cheese sauce. In a pan over medium-low heat, melt light butter. Add evaporated milk and light cream cheese; stir until smooth. Add cheddar and seasonings; stir until melted and creamy. Loosen with pasta water if needed.
  7. Combine and divide. Fold drained macaroni into the cheese sauce. Divide into 4 meal-prep containers or bowls; top each with a quarter of the hot-honey chicken. Store chicken separately if you want to re-crisp in the air fryer later.

Tips

  • Drip off excess egg before the cornflour so the coating stays thin and crisp, not gummy.
  • Don’t overcrowd the air-fryer basket — cook in batches if needed.
  • For meal prep, keep glazed chicken and mac separate; reheat chicken in the air fryer to restore crunch.
  • Adjust hot sauce vs honey to dial heat vs sweetness.
