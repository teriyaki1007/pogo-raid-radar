Tomato Beef Soup (罗宋汤)

~6–8 servings ~25 min prep ~2–3 hr cook No added water Method estimated

A deep red, concentrated tomato-beef soup (罗宋汤) built without added water — tomatoes and cabbage release the broth under a chuck roast until the beef is fork-tender and the pot smells like Sunday.

Ingredients

From the caption (exact list).

  • 1–2 lb beef chuck roast
  • 1 can whole tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 fresh tomatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 stalks celery
  • 1 head cabbage
  • 2 small potatoes
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

Approximate — reconstructed from video overlays.

  1. Prep the vegetables. Roughly chop cabbage, onion, celery, carrot, potatoes, and fresh tomatoes. Keep pieces large enough to hold up through a long simmer.
  2. Layer for a waterless broth. In a heavy pot, layer tomato and cabbage under the beef chuck so they sit against the heat and release liquid as they cook. Nestle remaining vegetables and the canned whole tomatoes around and over the beef. Do not add water — the broth comes from the tomatoes and cabbage.
  3. Cover and cook low and slow. Cover the pot. Cook over low heat (or in a low oven) until the beef is very tender and the soup has concentrated into a rich tomato broth — typically a few hours, depending on roast size and heat. Check occasionally that vegetables aren’t scorching; if needed, gently stir without breaking the beef apart early.
  4. Finish. Stir in the butter. Season generously with salt and pepper. Taste — the soup should be savory, a little sweet from tomatoes, and deeply concentrated.
  5. Serve. Slice or shred the beef into bowls and ladle the vegetable-rich broth over. Serve hot.

Tips

  • Trust the tomatoes and cabbage — they release more liquid than you’d expect once covered.
  • A heavy Dutch oven helps prevent scorching during the long covered cook.
  • Butter at the end rounds the acidity of the tomatoes.
