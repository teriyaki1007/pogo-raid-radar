Crispy Edamame Cucumber Salad

~2 servings ~10 min prep ~8–12 min cook ~20 min total Quantities estimated

Cool cucumber and creamy avocado under crispy pan-fried or air-fried edamame, with red onion, herbs, and a limey dressing — crunchy, green, and weeknight-fast.

Source note. Inspired by an overall.eats Instagram reel. Official amounts were gated in the caption — estimates below aim to match the look of the dish. Comment RECIPE on the reel for the creator’s version.
Approximate / not an official recipe. Reconstructed from reel visuals (cucumber, crispy edamame, avocado, red onion, lime, herbs). Adjust crunch, salt, and dressing to taste.

Ingredients

Estimates for 2 servings.

Salad

  • 2 large cucumbers, sliced into half-moons or thin ribbons
  • 1½ cups shelled edamame (thawed if frozen)
  • 1 ripe avocado, cubed
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro or mint (or a mix), roughly chopped
  • 1–2 scallions, thinly sliced (optional)

Crispy edamame

  • 1 Tbsp neutral oil (or spray for air fryer)
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder (optional)
  • Pinch of chili flakes or black pepper

Lime dressing

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil or toasted sesame oil (or half-and-half)
  • 1½ Tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tsp soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Prep the cool base. Slice cucumbers and red onion; cube avocado; chop herbs. Arrange cucumber in a wide bowl and keep avocado ready to add last so it stays neat.
  2. Crisp the edamame. Pat dry thoroughly. Toss with oil, salt, garlic powder, and chili. Pan: medium-high in a nonstick skillet, shake/stir 6–8 minutes until blistered and lightly browned. Air fryer: 190°C / 375°F for 8–12 minutes, shaking once, until crisp at the edges.
  3. Whisk the dressing. Stir oil, lime, soy, honey, and ginger until glossy. Taste for salt and acid.
  4. Assemble. Add red onion and herbs to the cucumber; drizzle most of the dressing and toss. Top with warm crispy edamame and avocado cubes. Finish with remaining dressing and scallions. Serve right away while edamame is still crunchy.

Tips

  • Dry edamame well — surface moisture is the enemy of crisp.
  • Add avocado last so it doesn’t mash into the toss.
  • Leftover crispy edamame re-crisps 2–3 minutes in a hot pan.
