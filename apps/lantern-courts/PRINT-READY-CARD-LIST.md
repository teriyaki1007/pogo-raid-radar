# Lantern Courts — Print-Ready Card List

**Aftermyth Series 1** · Set code suggestion: **LC01**

Series 1 starter / expansion pool — classical East Asian spirit courts. Favor, Seals, and the Mandate.

---

## Front Matter

### Resources & Stats
- **Favor** — Primary resource (analogous to mana). Spent to play Spirits, activate abilities, and cast Charms.
- **Might** — Combat power (analogous to power). There is no toughness; damage exhausts or defeats Spirits per house rules.

### Keywords
- **Seal** — A court mark placed on a Site or Spirit. Collecting Seals advances **Mandate** progress toward victory conditions.
- **Pin** — Locked to its current lane; cannot change lanes while pinned.
- **Exhaust** — Turned sideways / unavailable. Exhausted Spirits generally cannot attack or use tap-style abilities until recovered.
- **Open (Site)** — A Site brought into play face-up and active. Opening typically grants **+1 Favor** plus the Site's unique effect.
- **Mandate progress** — Track Seals and court dominance; fulfilling Mandate conditions wins the match (house rules).

### Rarity Codes
| Code | Rarity |
|------|--------|
| **C** | Common |
| **U** | Uncommon |
| **R** | Rare |
| **M** | Mythic |

### Courts
- **Celestial Bureau** — Seals, Favor, information, and destiny rewritten as paperwork.
- **Yokai Alleys** — Tricks, discard, Charms, masks, and night-parade aggression.
- **Mountain Kin** — Sites, exhaustion, taxes of the pass, and midrange resilience.

## Sites & Stadiums (LC01-S01 – LC01-S10)

Sites use **Open** mechanics (+1 Favor and a unique effect). Stadiums are legendary field cards — only one Stadium may be active at a time.

### LC01-S01 Bureau Gatehouse
**Site** — Celestial Bureau

Open — Gain 1 Favor. Place a Bureau Seal on Bureau Gatehouse.
(Sites remain open until closed or exhausted.)

*Cross after the third lantern and you owe the Bureau a name.*

### LC01-S02 Heaven's Ledger Hall
**Stadium** — Celestial Bureau

Legendary Field — Only one Stadium may be active.
Spirits you control get +1 Might for each Seal on Sites you control, to a maximum of +2.
Whenever you place a Seal, you may draw a card. If you do, discard a card.

*Floating ink revises lives while clerks pretend not to listen.*

### LC01-S03 Cloud Bridge of Stars
**Stadium** — Celestial Bureau

Legendary Field — Only one Stadium may be active.
Pay 1 Favor less to activate Pin abilities. (Minimum 0.)
Spirits you control with Pin abilities get +1 Might.

*Qiulan walks it alone; others fall through the gaps between stars.*

### LC01-S04 Night Market of Masks
**Site** — Yokai Alleys

Open — Gain 1 Favor.
Once each turn, while Night Market of Masks is open: Discard a card, then draw a card.

*Buy a mask, sell a memory, leave before your reflection puts one on.*

### LC01-S05 River Court Shallows
**Stadium** — Yokai Alleys

Legendary Field — Only one Stadium may be active.
Undamaged Spirits you control get +1 Might.
Charm spells cost 1 less Favor to cast if they target an undamaged Spirit. (Minimum 0.)

*The Kappa holds court here; courtesy is the first trap.*

### LC01-S06 Broken Torii Path
**Stadium** — Yokai Alleys

Whenever a Site opens or closes, Spirits you control get +1 Might until end of turn.
